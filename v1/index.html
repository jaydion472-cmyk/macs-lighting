FAQ

Good questions.

What are permanent outdoor lights?

They're LED lights mounted in a slim track along your roofline that stay up all year. You control the color, brightness and effects from a phone app, so you can switch from Christmas to game day to a warm everyday glow in seconds.

What's the difference between permanent and seasonal lighting?

Permanent lighting stays up year-round and is app-controlled. Seasonal lighting is a traditional holiday display we install, maintain, take down and store for you each year. Many customers start with one and add the other.

Can you see permanent lights during the day?

Barely. The lights sit in a low-profile channel that blends with your trim, so from the street they disappear until you turn them on.

Are permanent lights worth it?

If you decorate for more than one occasion a year, or you're tired of climbing ladders in the cold, most people find they pay off quickly in time, safety and curb appeal.

Can every roofline be lit?

Almost always. Steep pitches, tall peaks and odd angles are exactly why professional install matters. We'll walk your home and tell you exactly what's possible.

Will the lights bother my neighbors?

Brightness is fully adjustable, and schedules can turn lights off at whatever time you like. Many customers run a soft warm white in the evening and save the bright colors for special nights.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use the app?

No. We connect everything to your Wi-Fi, load your favorite scenes, and walk you through it before we leave.

How much does it cost, and what's in a quote?

Price depends mostly on the length of your roofline and the products you choose. Every quote is free with no obligation and covers lights, track, controller, installation and setup, so there are no surprises.

When should I book?

As early as you can. Our holiday schedule fills up fast every fall. Permanent installs can be done almost any time of year.
